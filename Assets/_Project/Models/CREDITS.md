# Model credits

CommandConsole: the floating command console that shows the wave titles and the countdown, generated by the developer with Meshy AI. A Blender script stood it up and reduced it from 856 thousand to 34 thousand triangles so the Quest can draw it, and its metallic and roughness maps were packed into the single mask texture the URP Lit shader expects.

ResultsBoard: the "TOWER DOWN" scoreboard of the results screen, generated by the developer with Meshy AI from their own reference image. Reduced the same way, from 479 thousand to 36 thousand triangles. Its material glows with its own colors and ignores the reflections of the game's sky, because it is shown in the player's real room.
