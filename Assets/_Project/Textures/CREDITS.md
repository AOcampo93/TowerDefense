# Texture credits

Material textures from ambientCG (https://ambientcg.com), public domain (Creative Commons Zero, CC0 1.0). Credit is not required but is given here with thanks.

* ElevatorSteel = Metal009, brushed stainless steel (1K)
* CabinFloor = DiamondPlate008C (1K)
* City/CityWall = Concrete017, City/CityRoof = Gravel017 (1K, color only)

Each set keeps the color and the OpenGL normal map. The MetallicSmoothness map packs the metalness (red channel) and the inverted roughness (alpha channel), the layout the URP Lit shader expects.

Sky/Sky_Dusk.jpg (and its small copy Sky_Reflection.jpg): "The Sky Is On Fire" by Greg Zaal and Rico Cilliers from Poly Haven (https://polyhaven.com/a/the_sky_is_on_fire), CC0, tonemapped and resized to 4096 x 2048. The buildings, mountain and lamp posts on its horizon were painted out and the colors were graded towards blue and violet.

Smoke_Puff.asset, Hazard/Hazard_Color.png (the yellow and black safety stripes on the platform edge) and Clouds/Clouds_Noise.png (tileable noise for the cloud layer below the tower) and Clouds/Clouds_Cumulus.png (four storm cloud shapes for the ring around the horizon) are generated by the project's setup scripts.

Clouds/Clouds_Illustrated.png: five illustrated clouds made by the developer, each one already cut out, cleaned of stray specks and packed into a 3 x 2 atlas by a setup script (the last cell is empty).

UI/GameLogo.png: the game logo, made by the developer. A setup script cut it out of its white background (including the gaps between the cables and the frame) and scaled it to 2048 pixels wide.
